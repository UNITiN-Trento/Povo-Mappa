# Povo Mappa

Povo Mappa helps people locate classrooms and understand their availability within UniTrento’s Polo Ferrari buildings.

## Language

**Room lookup**:
Locating a known classroom by its code or name.
_Avoid_: Room discovery, specific-room discovery

**Availability discovery**:
Finding an unknown classroom that is suitable because it is free at a relevant time.
_Avoid_: Room lookup, room discovery
